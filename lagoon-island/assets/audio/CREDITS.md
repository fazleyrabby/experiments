# Audio credits

All source recordings are CC0. They were trimmed, looped, level-matched, and re-encoded for this scene.

- `waves.mp3`: "Beach Ocean Waves" by jasinski, https://opengameart.org/content/beach-ocean-waves
- `jungle.mp3`: "Ambient Bird Sounds" by isaiah658, https://opengameart.org/content/ambient-bird-sounds
- `crickets.mp3`: "Crickets Ambient Noise - loopable" by Wolfgang_, https://opengameart.org/content/crickets-ambient-noise-loopable
- `cicada.mp3`: "Chorus Cicada singing" by Siobhan Leachman, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chorus_Cicada_singing.ogg

The breeze bed and the click-on-water drop are generated in code.
