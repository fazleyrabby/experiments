# Optimus Prime transformation studio

Reading: a cinematic 3D showcase for recording and sharing. Native CSS, plain JavaScript, Three.js and GSAP. The local Gundam reference establishes a centered mechanical subject, dark studio and two unobtrusive controls.

DESIGN_VARIANCE: 3. MOTION_INTENSITY: 9. VISUAL_DENSITY: 2 for the interface; detail belongs in the model. Aesthetic: cinematic product studio, not a component design system. Dark theme is explicit in the brief. Red and blue are the character's armor, cyan is emitted light, and controls use neutral silver. One 3px corner scale, no overlays or headings on the visible scene.

The procedural 3D model is the visual asset. No raster backdrop or generated mockup is needed. Rigid cab panels, nested limb pivots, visible chassis, axles and pistons form a reversible mechanical sequence. Camera motion provides storytelling and an ending turntable. Manual orbit interrupts the cinematic camera. Reduced motion opens on the final robot and makes mode changes immediate. Narrow screens frame the full model with controls visible.
