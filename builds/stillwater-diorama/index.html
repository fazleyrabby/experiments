Growing a little world

Arrow keys rotate the scene. W, A, S and D move around it; hold Shift to move faster. Plus and minus zoom. R resets the view. C changes the weather. Space pauses or resumes the environment. Click the water to make ripples. Two fingers pinch to zoom and drag to pan on touchscreens.
